How each report was placed

  1. One-day move. The desk marked these in desk-notes.json. A day/month swap never moves a date by one day. In these reports the date moves by one day, or people in two time zones see two different days for one meeting. They go to their own ticket, not under the tenant’s cause.
  2. Hint. The report came in through the booking form, and the tenant runs booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY to tenants who write the day first, so people type the month first as told.
  3. Parser. Everything else from a day-first tenant: the shared parser reads the date month first.

The cause comes from the tenant and the way the report came in (causes.ts), not from intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser locale.
